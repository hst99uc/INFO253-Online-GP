When you need to be seen in person

Some things can't be done safely over video. If your concern is on this list, see a local clinic or urgent care centre.

  • Chest pain, trouble breathing, or signs of stroke: call 111
  • Serious injuries, deep cuts or suspected broken bones
  • Vaccinations and immunisations
  • Cervical screening and other physical examinations
  • New prescriptions for controlled medicines, such as strong pain relief or ADHD medication
  • Driver licence medicals and pre-employment medicals
  • Babies under 3 months with a fever
  • Stitches, dressings and removing skin lesions
